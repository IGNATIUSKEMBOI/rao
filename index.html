Welcome to Moi Tambach

A Place Where Every Child Finds Their Potential

Founded in the heart of Tambach, Elgeyo Marakwet County, Moi Tambach Primary & Junior School has been a beacon of quality education for over 30 years.

Our dedicated staff, modern facilities and holistic CBC-aligned curriculum prepare students not just academically, but as well-rounded individuals ready for the 21st century.

Principal's Message

"Inspiring minds for a brighter tomorrow"

Welcome to our school community. At our institution, we are committed to providing a holistic and quality education that nurtures not only academic excellence but also character, creativity, and critical thinking. We believe that every learner is unique and full of potential, and it is our responsibility to create a supportive and inclusive environment where each student can thrive. Our dedicated team of teachers works tirelessly to inspire curiosity, encourage innovation, and instill strong values in our learners. We aim to equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence they need to face the challenges of an ever-changing world. We also recognize the importance of partnership between the school, parents, and the wider community. Together, we can ensure that our students grow into responsible, respectful, and successful individuals. Thank you for being part of our journey. We look forward to shaping a bright future together. Warm regards, Principal
